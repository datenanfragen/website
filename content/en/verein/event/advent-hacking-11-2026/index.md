It's been two years since our last contributor meetup—high time for the next one! Given the season, we want to meet up with you all for some cozy advent hacking. Regardless of whether you are already contributing to datarequests.org, whether you are interested in joining, or you just want to get to know us: Our contributor meetup is meant to be a space to ask questions on participating, for learning on how you can contribute to datarequests.org and Tweasel, and where data protection nerds can network on various issues. To take part, you need nothing in particular. Just be in our online meeting on November 29, 2026 from 16:00 (CET) and bring your curiosity, questions and motivation.
